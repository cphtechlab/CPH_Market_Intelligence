# 🇩🇰 Danish Market Intelligence API

Welcome to the definitive, all-in-one API for integrating with the Danish market. Whether you are building an international e-commerce platform, a global logistics routing engine, a FoodTech delivery app, or conducting market analysis, our API provides fast, reliable, and beautifully formatted JSON data sourced directly from official Danish government registries.

We handle the complex legacy XML feeds, government rate-limits, and obscure public data portals so you don't have to. You get clean, modern REST endpoints.

## 🚀 The Features (Everything is included)

### 📍 Geographical & Logistics Data (DAWA)
Perfect for E-commerce checkout validation, fleet management, and CRM enrichment.
* **Address Validation:** Search and auto-complete valid Danish addresses in milliseconds.
* **Reverse Geocoding:** Convert exact GPS coordinates (Lat/Lon) into official Danish street names, zip codes, and municipalities.
* **Postal Intelligence:** Validate zip codes and retrieve bounding boxes and municipality mapping.

### ⚡ Energy & Sustainability (Energi Data Service)
Essential for Smart Home apps, ESG reporting, and energy-aware routing.
* **Spot Prices:** Get hourly Day-Ahead electricity spot prices for Eastern and Western Denmark.
* **CO2 Emissions:** Track real-time CO2 emissions (g/kWh) in the Danish power grid.

### 🍔 FoodTech Hygiene Ratings (The "Smiley" API)
A must-have for Food Delivery Apps (UberEats, Wolt, JustEat) and review platforms (Yelp, TripAdvisor).
* **Smiley Data:** We cache the massive Danish Veterinary and Food Administration database, allowing you to instantly search restaurants by VAT Number (CVR) or Name to retrieve their official hygiene rating and inspection URL.

### 💼 Enterprise Macro & Finance ("The Big Three")
Built for ERP systems, market analysts, and global payroll software.
* **Official Exchange Rates:** Daily updated exchange rates directly from the Danish Central Bank (Nationalbanken).
* **Macroeconomics (CPI):** Access the official Consumer Price Index (Inflation) data from Statistics Denmark.
* **Bank Holidays:** Pre-calculated, 100% accurate official Danish public holidays and bank closures for any given year. 

## 🛠️ Why use CPH Market Intelligence?
* **Clean JSON:** We transform legacy governmental XML feeds into modern JSON.
* **Single Integration:** One API key gives you access to 4+ different government sectors.
* **High Performance:** Heavy data (like the Smiley registry) is cached in-memory on our enterprise-grade servers for O(1) response times.
* **Fully Compliant:** All data originates from open public sector data (Open Data DK).
